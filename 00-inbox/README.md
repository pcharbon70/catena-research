# Inbox (`00-inbox`)

## Purpose

The inbox holds captures that have not yet been classified or developed.

## What belongs here

Put temporary excerpts, rough questions, unprocessed links, and incomplete
notes here. Promote valuable material into a durable document kind during
review; do not let the inbox become permanent storage.
