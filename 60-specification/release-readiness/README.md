# Release Readiness (`60-specification/release-readiness`)

## Purpose

The normative release classes, evidence manifest, proof ledger, platform gate,
contradiction audit, and claim boundary under [Specification Authority](../../SPECIFICATION-AUTHORITY.md),
[Catena Conformance Vocabulary](../../CONFORMANCE-VOCABULARY.md), and
[Catena Implementation Limits and Portability](../../IMPLEMENTATION-LIMITS.md).

## What belongs here

Rules for deciding which release claim the available normative, executable,
empirical, and formal evidence supports. Public vocabulary and grammar remain
owned by P107 and P109.

## Variability register

Implementations may issue an experimental release with disclosed open items
and may decline to issue any release. They must not call a release complete or
stable without every applicable witness, and assessment never publishes or
upgrades a release automatically.

## Maintaining this index

Keep the readiness assessor, proof registry, checklist status, contradiction
audit, conformance traceability, support matrix, and evidence identities in
sync. Admit an integrated theorem only through a separately reviewed exact
proof-registry change.
