# Elixir v1.6 Released: The Code Formatter

## Reference

José Valim. Elixir v1.6 released. Elixir official blog, 2018-01-17. [Canonical source](https://elixir-lang.org/blog/2018/01/17/elixir-v1-6-0-released/).

Reading locations: Code formatter.

## Method

An official release article explains the design goals and rollout of mix format.

## Findings

The formatter aims to provide a common layout, reduce styling work, support learning through structural feedback, and focus review on behavior. The article also recommends breaking complex code into simpler expressions.

## Limits

These are the author's design aims and practical guidance, not measured causal effects on productivity or satisfaction.

## Relevance

Prototype Catena grammar together with its canonical formatter. A compact form that becomes confusing at realistic line lengths is a poor adoption candidate.
