# A Replication Study on the Intuitiveness of Programming Language Syntax

## Reference

Vilma Lappi, Ville Tirronen, Jonne Itkonen. A Replication Study on the Intuitiveness of Programming Language Syntax. Software Quality Journal 31, 1211–1240, 2023. [Canonical source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11219-023-09631-7).

Reading locations: Sections 3–6; especially participant selection in 3.3 and limitations in 6.

## Method

The replication of Stefik and Gellenbeck's keyword-rating study analyzes 153 Finnish-speaking participants: 90 programmers and 63 non-programmers. Participants rated candidate words and symbols for eleven concepts.

## Findings

Most original patterns replicated. Experience affected perceived intuitiveness; the change of native language had limited apparent influence in this relatively English-proficient sample.

## Limits

This replicates ratings, not the separate six-language code-writing experiment. Self-selected university participants and strong English proficiency do not establish accessibility across language backgrounds.

## Relevance

Separate prior familiarity from novice prediction. Treat word ratings as candidate-screening evidence, followed by actual writing, transfer, and repair tasks.
