# Do Be Do Be Do

## Reference

Sam Lindley, Conor McBride, Craig McLaughlin. Do Be Do Be Do. POPL 2017, 500–514, 2017. [Canonical source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.09259); [text consulted](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.09259).

Reading locations: Sections 1–2, especially 2.2–2.3; formal elaboration and typing sections. Read arXiv v2, 3 October 2017.

## Method

Frank combines language examples, a typed core elaboration, operational semantics, and a soundness account.

## Findings

Operators generalize functions into handlers; source effect polymorphism propagates ambient abilities. Empty ability brackets can mean the ambient ability rather than purity.

## Limits

This is formal and implementation evidence for Frank, not a participant study. Its economy of notation depends on a different semantic system.

## Relevance

Use Frank as a counterexample to assuming short signatures preserve Catena's meaning. Removing effect variables from source requires a justified inference or elaboration rule.
