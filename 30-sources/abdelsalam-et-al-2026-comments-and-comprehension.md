# The Effect of Comments on Program Comprehension: An Eye-tracking Study

## Reference

Youssef Abdelsalam, Norman Peitek, Annabelle Bergum, Sven Apel. The Effect of Comments on Program Comprehension: An Eye-tracking Study. Empirical Software Engineering 31, Article 94, 2026. [Canonical source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-025-10721-2).

Reading locations: Sections 4.3–4.8, 6.1, 7.1, and 8. Publication date: 14 March 2026.

## Method

Twenty computer-science students interpreted Java snippets with or without comments in a within-subject design. Measures included correctness, time, gaze, and reported perceptions.

## Findings

Comment effects varied across snippets; the combined performance score ranged from a decrease to an increase. Participants' appreciation of comments did not imply uniformly better measured comprehension.

## Limits

The sample is small, snippets were adapted and identifiers obfuscated, and the composite score combines time and correctness. These are not direct effect-row or IDE-hint results.

## Relevance

Treat explanatory prose and inferred-type displays as interfaces to test. Do not fill every line with help text and assume the result is easier to understand.
