# Progress and Preservation

## Purpose

This map routes the P132 question — the remaining progress and
preservation targets — through the archive's decision trail. The
normative answer is revision `0.1.45` in the [Progress and
Preservation Specification](../60-specification/progress-and-preservation/README.md).

## The route

1. **The component statements.** [Metatheory](../60-specification/data-and-patterns/metatheory.md)
   (C002) and C003's metatheory chapter fix the data- and
   condition-era targets that set the completion bar.
2. **The kernel evidence.** [Kernel
   Metatheory](../60-specification/formal-semantic-kernel/metatheory.md)
   holds sequential preservation and progress and mailbox
   preservation — standing evidence the process conditional
   restates.
3. **The failure terminal.** [The Six
   Categories](../60-specification/runtime-failure-taxonomy/the-six-categories.md)
   fixes `trap` as the terminal the effects-and-failure targets
   name; [the observability model](../60-specification/resource-observability/the-observability-model.md)
   keeps the survivor's world untouched.
4. **The agreement methodology.** [Trace-Observable
   Order](../60-specification/evaluation-order/trace-observable-order.md)
   (C030) is the witnessing discipline the new targets reuse.
5. **The contract.** The [Progress and Preservation
   Specification](../60-specification/progress-and-preservation/README.md):
   the effects-and-failure targets, the composed integrated theorem
   with its routed proof obligation, the conditional extensions,
   and conformance.
6. **The reasoning and decision record.** [Catena Progress and
   Preservation](../20-notes/catena-progress-and-preservation.md)
   argues the composed-statement middle; the [resolved
   inquiry](../40-inquiries/what-progress-and-preservation-targets-remain.md)
   preserves the forks.
