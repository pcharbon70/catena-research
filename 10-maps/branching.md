# Branching

## Scope

This map connects the C002 match-and-coverage contract, the C003
condition fragment, C010's commitment dynamics, and the C029–C032
sibling run this completes, to the C033 decision artifacts — match as
the only branch form, the conditional sugar promise, the consolidated
rule table, the no-statement absence — and the owners of termination,
failure, future scrutinee types, and spellings.
