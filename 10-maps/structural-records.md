# Structural Records

## Scope

This map connects the kernel's record and variant calculus, C002's
nominal exclusions, C030's order rows, C033's variant matching, and
C037's semantic maps to the C041 decision artifacts — the operation
table, kernel rows verbatim, the representation clause — and the
owners of spellings, collections, aliases, and refutability.
