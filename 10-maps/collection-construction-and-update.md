## Scope

This map connects C040's library-territory classification and C041's
records-not-collections boundary to C035's comparable set, C036's
taxonomy, and C037's representation invisibility, the C042 decision
artifacts — the six-topic routing, miss as value, the complexity
exclusion — and the owners of declarations, miss types, and
spellings.
